1 Introduction
In one-class classification, the goal is to characterize the distribution of nominal data to detect outliers.
Standard Null Foley-Sammon Transform (NFST) approaches often rely on heuristic partitioning, such asK-
means, to generate pseudo-labels. However, these methods assume hyperspherical clusters and fail to capture
complex, non-convex manifold structures. We propose a **Topological Manifold Partitioning (TMP)**
approach that discovers the natural connectivity of the data using graph-based contraction, extracting stable
connected components as the basis for the NFST subspace.
2 Topological Manifold Partitioning (TMP)
Instead of selecting anchors or cluster centers, we build a global representation of the data and decompose
it into topologically consistent components.
